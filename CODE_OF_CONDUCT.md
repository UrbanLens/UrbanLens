# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The adaptation shortens the text, names the reporting channel used by this project, and drops the Covenant's contact address.

## Our commitment

We want participation in UrbanLens, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions, plugins or the people who run their own instances, to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps build a respectful community includes:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Putting the community's shared interests first.

Behaviour that is not acceptable includes:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical address, without their explicit permission.
- Sharing the location of a place someone has asked to keep private, or encouraging others to visit places in ways that harm people, property or the places themselves.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces (the repository, its issues and pull requests, and project discussions), and wherever a person represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct that violates this Code through the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): GitHub's private reporting for this repository, or the maintainers directly if private reporting is unavailable. Include what happened, where, and any evidence you have.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers decide how to respond to a violation, and may use these steps in order or skip to any of them, depending on severity:

1. **Correction.** A private written notice explaining what was inappropriate, and a request to stop.
2. **Warning.** A notice of the consequences of continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban.** Removal from project spaces for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from project spaces.

Maintainers may also remove, edit or hide comments, commits or other contributions that do not follow this Code.
