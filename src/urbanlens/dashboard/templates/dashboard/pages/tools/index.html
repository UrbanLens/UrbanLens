Find Pins in a Photo Folder

Scan a folder of photos or videos on your device for geotagged locations. Nothing is uploaded while scanning - your files are only seen by your browser. When you're ready, upload any locations found to turn them into pins you can review. Only location and date metadata is uploaded by default - the photos themselves stay on your device unless you check one below to include it as a preview in your review queue. Your browser's folder picker may say "Upload" and warn about uploading files - that's standard browser wording for letting a site read local files, not something this tool controls. No files are sent anywhere until you click "Upload results" below.
