UrbanLens is open-source software. The people who run this site set its privacy practices, and this policy describes how the software handles your information. Your content is private by default and only visible to people you choose to share it with. Read the full policy below for the details.

{# -- Full policy -------------------------------------------------------------- #}
1

Who this policy covers

{% block privacy_scope %}

This policy explains what information this site collects when you use it, why, and how it's stored and shared. It applies to everyone who creates an account or otherwise uses it. See also our Terms of Service.

{% endblock %}
2

Information you give us directly

{% block privacy_direct_info %}

When you create an account, the site stores your username, email address, and password (hashed, never in plain text). As you use it, the site stores the content you create: pins, notes, photos, trip plans, wiki edits, messages, reviews, and similar content. That content stays private to you unless you explicitly share it.

{% endblock %}
3

Signing in with Google or Discord

{% block privacy_oauth %}

If you sign in using Google or Discord instead of a password, that provider shares basic profile information with the site to create and authenticate your account - typically your name, email address, and profile picture from Google, or your username, user ID, and email address from Discord. The site uses this information only to set up and sign you in.

{% endblock %}
4

Who can see your content

{% block privacy_visibility %}

Pins, notes, photos, and other content you create are private by default. Nothing you create is visible to another user until you take an explicit action to share it - by inviting a friend, posting to a trip, or otherwise changing a sharing setting. Even friend requests don't reveal whether an account exists until the request is accepted.

{% endblock %}
5

Information sent to research APIs

Many of the site's research tools (search, imagery, elevation, weather, wildlife data, historical records, and similar lookups) work by sending a location's coordinates or a place name to third-party APIs and returning what they know about that spot. These requests only ever include the coordinates or search terms needed to answer the lookup - never your name, account, or other identifying information. You can turn these lookups off entirely from onboarding or your account settings.

6

Connected photo services

{% block privacy_photo_services %}

If you connect a third-party photo service (such as Flickr, Immich, or Google Photos) to import media, the site stores the access token needed to talk to that service, encrypted at rest. It only uses that connection to import the photos you request, and you can disconnect a service at any time from your settings, which removes the stored token.

{% endblock %}
7

Cookies and tracking

{% block privacy_cookies %}

The site uses a session cookie to keep you signed in, a CSRF cookie to protect forms, and a short-lived signed cookie (ul_media) that lets your browser load your private photos and videos from the site's media host. Whoever runs the site decides whether to add analytics, advertising, or tracking. Ask them if that matters to you.

{% endblock %}
8

How we store and protect data

{% block privacy_storage %}

The people who run this site decide where and how the database is hosted, backed up, and protected. Sensitive values, like third-party API tokens, are encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but the software is designed to limit what a breach could expose - for example, by not collecting data it doesn't need in the first place.

{% endblock %}
9

How long we keep data

{% block privacy_retention %}

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings; deletion starts a 7-day grace period (during which you can undo it from Settings, or from the banner shown whenever you sign in), after which your account and its associated data are removed. Copies may remain in backups kept by the people who run this site until those backups are aged out.

{% endblock %}
10
{% block privacy_no_sale %}

Sharing your data

The people who run this site decide whether to share data beyond what this policy describes. Ask them if you want to know. The software itself sends data from the server only to the research and photo-import APIs you choose to use and to the sign-in providers you choose to authenticate with. Your browser also contacts other third parties directly to render pages: font hosts (Google Fonts), CDNs for some scripts and stylesheets (such as unpkg or cdnjs), map tile providers (such as Esri, OpenFreeMap, or Protomaps), and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoder, which receives the place names you type into a search box. Those providers see your IP address and the tiles, files, or search text requested. Whoever runs this site can serve the scripts and stylesheets from their own mirror; the other services are contacted regardless.

{% endblock %}
